# Restaurant Application 
Designed a restaurant ordering app to make discovering and ordering food effortless on mobile. The focus was on a visual menu, smart filtering, and a 3-step checkout to reduce friction. Built in Figma with a clean, high-contrast UI that works well in low-light and on the go.
A mobile-first restaurant ordering app designed to make discovering and ordering food effortless. The experience focuses on: - **Visual menu** for quick browsing - 
**Smart filtering** to find what you want fast - 
**3-step checkout** to reduce friction and speed up ordering ### Design Designed in 
**Figma** with a clean, high-contrast UI that works well in 
**low-light** and while on the go. ### Built with -
